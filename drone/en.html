Technology

An Integrated Sensor Architecture for Reliable Flight Without GPS

HUR-UAV-01 brings together multiple layers of sensing and rugged field engineering, so flight stays stable and precise even when satellite signal is unavailable.

01

GPS-Denied Navigation

Precision stabilization without relying on GPS

Optical Flow Camera

Tracks ground motion frame-by-frame, with an effective ceiling of up to 35 meters.

LiDAR Distance Sensor

High-speed altitude measurement from 0.1 m up to 40 m.

Improved EKF

Fuses optical flow, barometer and magnetometer data to reduce IMU drift and support position hold.

Multi-Sensor Fusion

Integrates camera, barometer, magnetometer and IMU data into a single decision model.

Immune to GPS Jamming

Stable flight with no reliance on any external signal.

Dual Operating Modes

Operate with GPS or in GPS-denied mode, switching according to the mission scenario.

02

AI Object Detection & Tracking

Intelligent visual awareness

Automatic Object Detection & Tracking

Integrated computer vision automatically detects and tracks targets, with reported confidence levels up to 75% depending on target, scene and model configuration.

* Actual detection and tracking performance varies by scene, target, lighting and field conditions.

03

Engineering & Reliability

Designed for demanding field conditions

Dual-IMU

Redundant inertial sensing for a more robust estimate of flight state.

STM32 Flight Controller

Fast embedded processing for real-time flight control.

Dual Vibration Isolation

Dedicated damping for the flight controller and guidance sensors.

Open-Source Hardware

A flexible, modifiable architecture — ideal for research and Teknofest projects.

Rapid Battery Swap

Integrated locking and quick-change workflow that maximizes real time in the field.

FAQ

Everything You Need to Know Before Reaching Out

Can the platform be customized for research or Teknofest projects?

Yes. The hardware is open-source by design, which allows the payload, software and navigation algorithms to be adapted to your research project or competition challenge.

Does it really fly without a GPS signal?

Yes — through the integration of optical flow, LiDAR, barometric data and an improved EKF, with the ability to switch between GPS and GPS-denied modes depending on the mission scenario.

How accurate is the AI object detection?

Detection and tracking confidence can reach up to 75%, depending on the target, scene and model configuration; actual performance varies by mission conditions.

How fast is delivery within Turkey?

Standard delivery is within 3 days of order confirmation.

Is technical support and training provided after purchase?

Yes, our engineering team provides technical support and training for operating teams, with a direct WhatsApp line available 24/7.

How do I get a quote for my institution or university?

Reach out via WhatsApp or the contact form below, and our team will respond with a tailored quote and technical consultation.
